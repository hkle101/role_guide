Overwatch Tank Guide

Control space, protect your team, start fights

Example Tanks

Reinhardt

Shield + brawl pressure

D.Va

Dive + peel with Matrix

Winston

Jump in, bubble, disrupt

Use your shield to cross dangerous sightlines, then swing when close.

Peel for your supports with Defense Matrix, and chase low targets safely.

Jump with your team ready to follow up, then bubble to cut healing/lines of sight.
Tank Tips

Don’t overextend — take space with your team.

Track your defensive cooldowns and play corners.
